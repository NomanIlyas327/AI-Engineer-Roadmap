Return, Exchange & Refund Policy:

1. Return Window: Aap delivery ke 7 din ke andar product return ya exchange ki request submit kar sakte hain.
2. Return Conditions: 
   - Product original condition mein hona zaroori hai.
   - Box, accessories, aur invoice (rased) sath honi chahiye.
   - Sealed items (jaise Earbuds) agar open kar diye gaye hon toh change-of-mind par return nahi honge.
3. Damaged or Defective Items: Agar product raste mein damage hua ho ya kharab nikle, toh 24 ghante ke andar WhatsApp support par video proof ke sath claim karein. Immediate free replacement di jaye gi.
4. Refund Process: 
   - Product receive aur inspect hone ke baad refund 3 se 5 working days mein process hota hai.
   - Refund JazzCash, EasyPaisa, ya Bank Transfer ke zariye bheja jata hai.
5. Non-Refundable Items: Clearance sale items aur physical damage kiye gaye products returnable nahi hain.
