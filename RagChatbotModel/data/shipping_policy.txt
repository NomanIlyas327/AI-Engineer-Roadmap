Shipping Policy & Delivery Timelines:

1. Delivery Coverage: Hum poore Pakistan mein delivery service provide karte hain.
2. Major Cities Delivery: Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, aur Faisalabad mein delivery 2 se 3 working days mein hoti hai.
3. Other Cities & Remote Areas: Baki tamam shehron aur door-daraaz ilaqon mein delivery 4 se 6 working days mein hoti hai.
4. Delivery Charges:
   - PKR 3,000 se ziada ki shopping par FREE Delivery hai.
   - PKR 3,000 se kam ki shopping par PKR 250 Flat Shipping Fee lagti hai.
5. Courier Partners: Humare official courier partners TCS aur PostEx hain. Order dispatch hone ke baad SMS par Tracking Number bhej diya jata hai.
6. Cash on Delivery (COD): COD facility tamam orders par available hai. COD orders par koi extra charges nahi hain.
